# Changelog

## Outlaws × Butchering 1.0.0 — first public release
- Humans of Outlaw Mod (10 kinds) can be butchered with Butchering. Each body gives long pork, fat, human hide, sinew and offal, and always exactly one head.
- Feral and hunting hounds can be butchered too. This used to be a separate private mod, "OutlawsButchering". Item codes `outlawsbutchering:deadhound-*` are kept, so existing saves are fine.
- Bodies have dead and hanging poses, go into the back slot and slow the player.
- Bodies from the creative inventory get default drops and a bone pile.
- Long pork can be cooked and cured, and goes into vanilla stews, soups and pies.


### Expanded Foods support (built in)
The former separate add-on "Outlaws × Butchering: Expanded Foods" is now part of this mod and turns on only when Expanded Foods is installed.
- Long pork works like bushmeat in Expanded Foods: extra states, oven, meat hook aging, nuggets, breaded meat, sausages, broth, pies.
- Optional support for EF: Meals, Sauce-a-lot and Sammiches. Each part turns on only when its mod is installed.
- Uses the bushmeat models of the other mods instead of copying them.
